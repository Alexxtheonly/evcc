# Energy metrics contract

`HomeForecast(from, to time.Time) (*HomeForecastResult, error)` returns consecutive absolute fifteen-minute `rates` with `start`, `low`, `base`, `high` in **Wh**. Pass `from` in the site's local timezone. The history is household energy after loadpoint demand has been removed, never grid import. Training ends strictly before `from`. A local day has 92 or 100 absolute intervals at DST changes, while historical day-type buckets use local clock time.

`quality` reports `source` (`day_type`, `pooled`, `interpolated`, `cached`, `unavailable`), `samples`, `rejectedSamples`, `coveredBuckets`, `coverage` (0–1), `missingBuckets` (0–95), `interpolatedBuckets`, `pooledBuckets`, `latestSample`, `lastGood`, `lastGoodAgeSeconds`, `reason`, `rangeSource`, `calibrationSamples`. Interpolation/pooling counts describe both day-type profiles combined. Observed bucket coverage never includes imputation. Weekdays and weekends use separate means with a fourteen-day recency half-life; fewer than two same-day-type observations uses the pooled bucket. At least 144 clean rows, 92 observed buckets and a sample within 48 hours are required. Only isolated missing buckets can be interpolated. A last valid profile can be reconstructed at a cutoff up to seven days earlier. This is labelled `cached` with its age and current failure reason. Recovered, incomplete, NULL, negative and nonfinite energy cannot become training data. A measured zero remains zero.

`ArchiveHomeForecast(issued, slots)` must receive the final forecast after caller-side corrections. It freezes predictions roughly 1, 6 and 24 hours ahead, retains ninety days, and does not overwrite an earlier issue. Error envelopes use only archived predictions issued before their matched clean observations. At least thirty past pairs per lead are required; otherwise ranges remain historical variation. These are empirical sensitivity ranges, not calibrated probability guarantees.

`SolarForecastRange(from, leadMinutes, baseWh)` returns low/high Wh, sample count and error. It reuses existing solar forecast archives, requires complete clean PV meter coverage, and excludes observations at or after the issue cutoff. Queries are cached per database and fifteen-minute cutoff. Before thirty pairs exist, the sensitivity range is 50–150% of the base forecast. Residuals come from the raw provider archive; a caller that has bias-corrected the base must label the resulting range as sensitivity rather than measured coverage.

`BatteryEfficiencyCandidates(from)` returns stable device `name`, optional charge/discharge efficiency, separate sample counts, rejected count, accepted observation dates, independent `capacityKWh`, `source`, `measurementPlane` and `applicable`. Twelve clean one-direction consecutive intervals per direction are required. Capacity is never fitted together with efficiency. Rounded SoC movement below two percentage points, mixed charge/discharge and implausible efficiency are rejected. `SetBatteryMeasurementPlane(name, "ac"|"dc"|"unknown")` records an explicit independent declaration. Only AC-plane candidates are applicable to the AC energy solver. Fronius GEN24 battery MPPT counters are DC; they cannot identify inverter conversion efficiency. Unknown and DC-plane candidates remain visible but never replace the assumed 0.9 efficiencies. Wear is a separate optional currency amount per **DC kWh discharged**, never inferred from warranty throughput.

`SaveOptimizerSnapshot(OptimizerSnapshot)` returns a uint64 ID. Version 1 snapshots freeze timestamp, controller version, request, result, economics and quality JSON. Each combined payload is capped at 512 KiB; retention is thirty days and at most 10,000 entries. `economics` has a `batteries` array of `SnapshotBatteryEconomics`: `name`, `capacityKWh`, `etaC`, `etaD`, `floorFrac`, `maxChargeKWh`, `maxDischargeKWh`, optional `wearPerKWh`, optional `measurementPlane`, `source`. `BindControlSlotSnapshot` and `BindOptimizerRunSnapshot` preserve first-observation identity. Pruning clears links and marks history unavailable; expired snapshots never become inferred legacy assumptions. SetupSchema migrates all tables. Delete routes should call `DeleteOptimizerSnapshots` and `DeleteHomeForecastSamples`; reset must include `optimizer_snapshots` and `home_forecast_samples`.

Each decision may expose `optimizerSnapshotId` and `outcome` with `status`, `reason`, `through`, `slots`, `cashDeltaEur`, `wearDeltaEur`, `terminalEnergyDeltaKWh`, `terminalValueDeltaEur`, `netDeltaEur`, `assumptionsSource`. **Positive deltas mean the applied decision cost more.** Both trajectories start at the same measured charge; only the first action differs. Subsequent recorded controls are held fixed. This is a conditional replay, not a fully reoptimized counterfactual. It stops when charge rejoins (`completed`), observations end (`pending`), or a gap, unrecorded mode change, unsupported action or changed economics interrupts it (`interrupted`). Missing initial evidence is `unpriced`. Replay is bounded to 48 hours. Net delta equals cash plus configured wear minus retained-inventory value. Terminal value assumes deliverable DC energy is worth the nonnegative final grid rate. Pending/interrupted estimates are unresolved, and overlapping decisions must never be summed. The existing `slotFlowDeltaEur` is only an immediate cash difference.

`chain.inventoryAdjusted` is the aggregate comparison: `status` (`estimated`, `partial`, `unavailable`), optional `reason`, `worlds`, `contributions`, `control`, `terminalAdjustment`, optional `wearAdjustment`, `segments`, `estimatedEndpoints`, `measurementCaveat`, `assumptionsSource`, `valuation`. World/contribution settlement shape remains `{perSlot, periodAverage}`. `control.full` is interval settlement and `control.routing` is period-average settlement. **Positive control values mean savings.** Both the ordinary-operation baseline and actual history are corrected for opening minus closing battery inventory. Gaps or changed physics create separately priced inventory-neutral segments, explicitly partial. Observed SoC endpoints take precedence over meter-energy estimates. All historical snapshots are honored; unsupported or expired economics is unavailable. Missing wear means excluded unknown cost. Legacy cash-only worlds stay available for inspection but do not include inventory correction.

Synthetic falsification cases are in `core/metrics/energy_intelligence_test.go`: buying 5 AC kWh for EUR 1 and recovering 4.5 kWh against EUR 1.80 later saves EUR 0.80; EUR 0.90 wear reverses the benefit. A baseline that consumes EUR 1.80 of opening inventory cannot receive EUR 1.80 free savings. The optional `EVCC_HOME_HISTORY_FIXTURE` check reads an external JSON array `{ts,energy}` without committing private household data.

The selected display period does not limit replay evidence: a decision at 23:45 can complete with the following day's recorded payoff while the view still returns only that original decision. Additional meter/control evidence extends up to 48 hours per decision and stops before the current, unfinished slot. The same explicitly supplied static feed-in fallback is validated for both the selected and subsequent periods. Missing subsequent evidence interrupts replay; it cannot become an invented zero.

Battery modes follow directional inverter limits: `hold` prevents discharge while allowing surplus charging; `holdcharge` prevents charging while allowing discharge against household deficit. The Fronius GEN24 template implements these respectively with SunSpec model 124 `StorCtl_Mod=2, OutWRte=0` and `StorCtl_Mod=1, InWRte=0`. Immediate and multi-slot replay share these semantics. Source-derived regressions and the cross-midnight payoff test are in `core/metrics/ledger_replay_boundary_test.go`.
